We've launched a blog. Delete this post once you've got a real one ready to replace it — it's only here so the site isn't empty and you can see how a post looks once it's live.

To add your own post, copy this file, rename it, and change the text above between the lines (the "front matter"):

- **title** — shows as the heading and in the browser tab
- **branch** — "Conisbrough", "Staveley", or "Both practices" — shows as the small tag above the title
- The filename must start with the date in `YYYY-MM-DD-` format, then a short title with hyphens instead of spaces, ending in `.md`

Everything below the second `---` is the post itself, written in plain text (Markdown). A blank line starts a new paragraph.
